The Undead

A zombie apocalypse begins with a rat that eats a corpse then pees into a man’s wound during a flood. Now the virus spreads fast, and the dead are walking. Caught in the chaos are three unlikely survivors: loudmouth Prince, hyper Jeffrey, and the overly dramatic Erika. Between blood, bites, and bad decisions, they’ll have to stick together to survive… or end up joining the undead themselves.
